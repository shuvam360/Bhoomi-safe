Landslide Precaution Guidelines

🌧️ High Rainfall Warning

If sustained rainfall exceeds 150mm/24h, avoid travelling through hill passes like NH-37 or Shillong Bypass.

🔊 Listen for Warning Sounds

Trees cracking, boulders knocking, or sudden increase in muddy stream flow indicate imminent landslide activity.

🚗 If Trapped in Vehicle

Abandon vehicle immediately if mud or debris starts moving onto the road and seek higher ground on foot.
